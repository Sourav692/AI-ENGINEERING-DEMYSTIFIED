# Ambiguity and Discovery - Practice Worksheet

**What this competency tests:** can you walk into a vague customer problem and define it yourself?

Four questions. Draft each one cold before revealing the key — a behavioural answer you have read is not an answer you can deliver. Fill the STAR fields in your own words, out loud first, then written.

### The four beats of a credible failure answer

Two of the questions below are failure-shaped. Airtime matters more than content:

| Beat | Airtime | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Own the call | ~10% | One sentence. No throat-clearing. |
| What you missed | ~40% | The beat candidates skip. Skipping it reads as evasion. |
| The correction | ~25% | What you actually did once you knew. |
| What changed | ~25% | The durable change. This is what separates senior from mid. |
