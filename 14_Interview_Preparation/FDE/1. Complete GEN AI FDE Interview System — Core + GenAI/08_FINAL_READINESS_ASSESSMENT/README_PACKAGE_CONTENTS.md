# GenAI FDE Final Readiness Package

This package replaces the earlier single-file self-assessment with an evidence-based readiness system.

## Canonical readiness standard

A candidate is interview-ready only when all conditions are satisfied:

- Overall score is at least 82/100.
- Every section minimum is met.
- All required evidence is submitted and verified.
- Required lab, coding, and mock-interview gates are complete.
- No automatic-fail condition is triggered.

## Files

1. `00_READ_BEFORE_TAKING_THIS_ASSESSMENT.pdf` - prerequisite gate and candidate declaration.
2. `01_FINAL_READINESS_CANDIDATE_EXAM.pdf` - timed 150-minute exam with three scenarios and executable-code requirements.
3. `02_DETAILED_SCORING_RUBRIC.pdf` - point allocation, section minimums, performance anchors, and automatic-fail rules.
4. `03_REVIEWER_GUIDE_AND_REFERENCE_ANSWERS.pdf` - scoring calibration and strong-answer framework; open only after the timed attempt.
5. `04_FINAL_EVIDENCE_CHECKLIST.pdf` - required proof and evidence-folder structure.
6. `05_RETAKE_AND_REMEDIATION_PLAN.pdf` - targeted remediation and retake rules.
7. `99_FINAL_READINESS_SCORECARD.xlsx` - formula-driven scoring, evidence, automatic-fail, and retake tracker.
8. `99_TAKE_LAST_FINAL_GENAI_FDE_READINESS_ASSESSMENT.md` - canonical entry-point notice replacing the old assessment.

Editable DOCX versions of the six PDF documents are included.
