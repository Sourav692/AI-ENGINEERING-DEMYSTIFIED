# Refund Policy

Support agents may answer refund questions using this policy.

Approved refund rules:

- Duplicate payments can be refunded after invoice verification.
- Service outage credits require incident confirmation and manager approval.
- Refunds above 10000 EUR require manager approval.
- Requests older than 90 days require legal review.
- A support agent must not promise a refund before verification.

Customer response guidance:

- Acknowledge the issue.
- Explain the verification step.
- State what evidence is needed.
- Avoid sharing internal approval thresholds unless the customer is already contractually entitled to them.
