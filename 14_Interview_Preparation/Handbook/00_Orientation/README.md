# Module 00 · Orientation

> **Level** 🟢 Foundations · **Docs** 3 · **Time** ~45 min total
> **Prerequisites:** none

Before any architecture, three things: how this handbook is built, which role you are preparing for, and the one habit that separates an engineer who designs the right system from one who designs *a* system.

| # | Doc | What you get | Time |

## Checkpoint

You are ready for Module 01 when you can:

- Name the four levels and say which modules belong to each.
- State the three lenses of a senior FDE's work and why they are interdependent rather than sequential.
- List the five question areas of the first ten minutes and give one question for each from memory.
