# Sales Playbook

Sales reps may summarize CRM health, renewal date, business risk, and approved support status when preparing for customer conversations.

Renewal risk should be escalated when one or more of the following are true:

- Health score is below 65.
- There are unresolved Sev1 or Sev2 support tickets.
- The customer champion has changed role or left the company.
- Security, legal, or procurement has requested additional evidence near renewal.

Recommended actions:

1. Confirm the business owner and technical champion.
2. Identify open support blockers.
3. Prepare a concise executive summary with sources.
4. Avoid promising roadmap features that are not approved.
5. Ask a manager to join if ARR is above 400000 EUR and risk is medium or high.
