## Valid interview-ready result

A candidate is classified as interview-ready only when all of the following are true:

- Overall score is at least **82/100**.
- Discovery score is at least **15/20**.
- Architecture score is at least **18/25**.
- Evaluation and security score is at least **15/20**.
- Production-debugging score is at least **11/15**.
- Coding score is at least **7/10**.
- Executive-communication score is at least **7/10**.
- All required evidence is present.
- Required lab, coding, and mock-interview gates are completed.
- No automatic-fail condition is triggered.

A score calculated without these requirements is not a valid readiness score.
