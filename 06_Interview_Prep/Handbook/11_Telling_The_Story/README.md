# Module 11 · Telling the Story

> **Level** 🔴 The FDE Role · **Docs** 2 + 2 worked narratives + 1 STAR rendering · **Time** ~1 h reading, then practice
> **Prerequisites:** Module 10; a project of your own to apply the formats to

You have built the systems. This module is how to talk about them — in a timed deep-dive, in an open conversation where the listener steers, and as a technical implementation flow — and the honesty discipline that makes anything you say believable: knowing, and saying, exactly what you have proven and where the demo ends.

## Checkpoint

You have finished the handbook when you can:

- Deliver a 15-minute deep-dive of a project of your own from segment headers alone, with the limitation offered before the close.
- Open a conversation with one sentence, stop, and deploy the right beat for whatever they ask next.
- Produce a coverage map for your project with "what to say" for every gap.
- Tell one war story from your own work with its lesson in one sentence.
