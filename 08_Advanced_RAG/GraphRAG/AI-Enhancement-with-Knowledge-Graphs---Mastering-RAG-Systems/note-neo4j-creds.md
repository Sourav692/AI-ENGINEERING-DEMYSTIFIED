# Neo4j Aura connection template

> Set up your own free Neo4j AuraDB instance at https://console.neo4j.io and fill in these values in your `.env` — do not commit real credentials here.

NEO4J_URI=neo4j+s://your-instance-id.databases.neo4j.io
NEO4J_USERNAME=neo4j
NEO4J_PASSWORD=your_neo4j_password_here
AURA_INSTANCEID=your_instance_id
AURA_INSTANCENAME=your_instance_name
